# Neko Nightfall - asset sources

## Current runtime assets

The player, zombies, district buildings, trees, planters, fences, props, and their materials are created in Godot scripts from primitive meshes and original material settings. The runtime does not depend on downloaded character or environment packs.

The survivor is an original anime-inspired ice-officer design. It may use broad genre cues such as long blue hair and a formal uniform, but must not reproduce Esdeath's exact costume, insignia, face, or other distinctive details.

## Future additions

Record each future third-party asset here with its author, source URL, license, attribution requirements, and the exact files used. Keep source/license files when redistribution requires them. Do not add complete packs or duplicate formats when the game only uses one optimized runtime file.

The visual target is an original atmospheric survival-horror game. Do not copy Resident Evil characters, logos, maps, sounds, textures, or other distinctive copyrighted material.
